Primary Exhibition Space

Located in the heart of Tenderloin, San Francisco, our flagship display space features a large street-visible TV mounted on a prominent storefront. This high-traffic location ensures that thousands of community members, visitors, and art enthusiasts pass by daily.

The space showcases rotating exhibitions of winning artworks from our contests. Each piece is displayed prominently, giving local artists unprecedented visibility and transforming the urban landscape into an open-air gallery.

🎨 The Winged Delegates Experience

Our Tenderloin space represents more than just a display location—it's a statement about art's role in community transformation. By placing visual art on a street-visible TV, we democratize access to creative expression and celebrate the artistic voices of our community.

Artists' work receives genuine public exposure, reaching audiences who might not typically visit traditional galleries. This is art where people live, work, and gather—making creativity an integral part of the urban fabric.
