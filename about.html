Our Story

Founded on Crisis, Built on Hope

Winged Delegates was born from tragedy and determination. In 2025, a residential fire devastated a multi-unit building in San Francisco's Tenderloin neighborhood. A faulty lithium-ion battery—from an e-scooter or e-bike—ignited without warning, displacing families and destroying homes. This wasn't an isolated incident. Over 100 similar fires occur annually in the Tenderloin alone.

Rather than accept this as inevitable, our community asked: How can we use our collective talents to prevent future fires? The answer came through art. Winged Delegates emerged as a space where creativity meets fire safety, where visual art becomes a tool for awareness and behavioral change.

We believe that art speaks louder than statistics. A powerful image, an emotional narrative, a striking visualization—these can inspire the choices that save lives. By displaying artworks on street-visible displays and digital platforms, we reach people in their daily lives with messages about battery safety, electrical device hazards, and community responsibility.

Our Core Values

🎨 Creativity First

We believe art in all its forms—digital, traditional, multimedia—has the power to move people and inspire change.

🤝 Community-Centered

This is not about us. It's about the residents, artists, schools, and organizations who join us in preventing fires.

🌟 Inclusivity

Everyone has a voice. We welcome artists of all ages, backgrounds, experience levels, and artistic styles.

🔥 Impact-Driven

Our ultimate goal is not just beautiful art, but behavioral change that prevents fires and saves lives.

Where We Display Your Art

📺 Tenderloin Street Display

Our primary display location is a street-visible TV in San Francisco's Tenderloin neighborhood. This high-traffic area is frequented by residents, workers, students, and visitors throughout the day. Your winning artwork will be featured on this prominent display, reaching thousands of people.

The display showcases rotating artworks, with each selected piece featured for a full month. We ensure adequate visibility and impact, with our display running during peak foot traffic hours.

🏘️ Location: Tenderloin District, San Francisco | Display Format: LED TV Screen | Audience: 1,000+ daily viewers
