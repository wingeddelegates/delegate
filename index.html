About the Initiative

Winged Delegates is a community-driven art initiative dedicated to discovering, celebrating, and showcasing exceptional visual artworks from emerging and established artists.

Our mission is to create a platform where artistic creativity thrives and reaches new audiences. Through regular contests and exhibitions, we bring art to life—literally displaying winning artworks on Tenderloin's street-visible TV, transforming public spaces into dynamic galleries.
