What is Winged Delegates?

A Community-Driven Art Initiative

Winged Delegates is a grassroots art initiative founded to address the critical need for fire safety awareness in urban communities. Our mission is to showcase compelling visual art that educates, inspires, and motivates people to adopt safer practices around electrical devices, batteries, and home fire prevention.

We display winning artworks on a street-visible TV in San Francisco's Tenderloin neighborhood and on our website, reaching thousands of residents and visitors who can benefit from the powerful messages embedded in these creative works.

The Crisis We're Addressing

⚠️ 100+ Fires Annually

Over 100 fires have been reported in San Francisco's Tenderloin neighborhood, many caused by faulty or improperly stored lithium-ion batteries and electrical devices. In 2025, a residential fire displaced families and destroyed homes. Most of these fires are preventable through awareness and behavioral change.

Winged Delegates believes that art can inspire the change our communities need.
