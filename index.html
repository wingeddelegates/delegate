About the Initiative

Winged Delegates is an art initiative built around a single street-facing window in San Francisco’s notorious Tenderloin. Born from a devastating fire and shaped by the neighborhood’s social challenges, this initiative opens a digital art contest around the very issues this community lives with every day.

We invite artists to create works that can resonate with the people passing by - not inside a gallery, but on the street, in real life. Winning works are sent ' winged' onto our screen, where they bring light, reflection, and positive energy to the very people most artworks never reach.

We are not a gallery. We are a delegation for change.

Space Features

High-Traffic Location

Positioned in a busy area with constant foot traffic, ensuring maximum visibility for displayed artworks.

Professional Display

State-of-the-art TV screen with excellent image quality and clarity for optimal artwork presentation.

Community Impact

Direct engagement with local residents and visitors, fostering meaningful connections between artists and audiences.

Rotating Exhibition

Monthly rotations ensure fresh content and provide opportunities for multiple artists throughout the year.

24/7 Availability

Artworks are displayed around the clock, reaching audiences at all times of day and night.

Accessibility

Located on a public street with wheelchair access, ensuring art is available to everyone in the community.
