Tenderloin Fire Safety Art Contest

Contest Purpose

In 2025, a devastating fire broke out in a multi-unit residential property in Tenderloin, caused by a faulty scooter or e-bike battery explosion on a hot day. This fire destroyed one unit completely and damaged multiple other units through smoke and water damage from firefighting efforts, displacing numerous families. More than 100 such fires have been reported in the Tenderloin area of San Francisco alone.

This art contest aims to inspire behavioral change and raise awareness about fire safety in our community. Tenderloin is a high-traffic area where local art can reach and inspire residents and visitors to adopt safer practices and prevent similar tragedies in Tenderloin, San Francisco, and beyond.

Submission Details

  • Deadline: August 30th, 2026
  • Art Format: We welcome diverse artwork formats in any medium that can inspire people and promote fire safety awareness
  • Awards: Approximately 1/3 of submissions will be selected for awards
  • Recognition: Award winners will receive a formal award letter sent to their school. Winning artwork will be displayed on this website or in street displays throughout Tenderloin
