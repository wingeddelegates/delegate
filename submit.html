Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit multiple artworks?

Yes! You may submit up to 3 different artworks. Each submission requires a separate form.

Is there an age limit?

No! We welcome submissions from all ages. Please indicate your age group or grade level.

When will winners be announced?

Winners announced by September 30, 2026. Award letters sent to schools and winners announced publicly.

Is there an entry fee?

No! Entry is completely free. This is a community initiative to promote fire safety.

What if my work doesn't win?

All submissions will be reviewed. We celebrate the creativity of all artists participating in our mission.

Is my personal info confidential?

Yes. Your information is confidential and used only for judging and winner notification.
